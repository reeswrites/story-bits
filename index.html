    Grammars & spaces

    Grammars

    A grammar is the rules of a piece: its cards, word lists, beat lines, what the dial clashes, and any constraints. Fork one to make it yours, share it by link, or suggest it for everyone. Yours stay in this browser.

    Spaces

    A space is a shared folder for a group: its grammars (every version), word packs, kept pieces, cast, lore and jam history. Jams opened in a space draw from it. Make one public to publish what's in it by a short link. Nothing goes to a space unless you put it there.

    ▸

    The cast

    People you've kept. Everything that has happened to them so far.

    Story Bits began with one rule for funny stories: things that happened that don't quite fit, landing somewhere it's safe to laugh. That rule is now one grammar among others. Nothing here needs an account; your grammars, words and cast stay in this browser unless you put them in a space.
