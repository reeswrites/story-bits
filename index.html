Story Bits

Something happened to someone, and they feel some way about it. You don't have to guess how they feel. That's the point. Tap any word to tune it, from plain to strange. Things can hide inside other things, and you can add words of your own.

    Mood

    The cast

    People you've kept. Everything that has happened to them so far.

    Funny stories are things that happened that don't quite fit, landing somewhere it's safe to laugh. Turn up the dial to widen the gap.
