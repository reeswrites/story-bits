# Phone QA sweep (2026-10-15)

Newcomer walk-through of the live site at https://reeswrites.com/story-bits/ in the Claude desktop built-in browser: 375×812 (touch emulation), 390×844, light and dark. Nothing in the app was changed. Test jams, spaces, forks and bank words were deleted afterwards.

**How to read this.** Severity: *blocker* = a newcomer can't continue or loses work; *annoying* = works but confuses, hides things or reads wrong; *polish* = small. No blockers were found. Screenshot descriptions say what the screen showed at that moment (screenshots are not stored in the repo).

**Caveats.**
- The first ~half of the sweep ran on an older cached build (picker without Sideways/Sound moves). I re-checked the picker, first visit, challenge, jam, spaces and history on the fresh build (`bcf1cb2`, assets `?v=bcf1cb256a33` and later). Where the two builds differ, the table says so.
- The embedded browser has no soft keyboard and refuses `navigator.clipboard` for synthetic taps, so "text hidden behind the keyboard" is **unverified** (see the first row of "Not verified").
- Automated contrast on the cream paper gave false 1.3:1 readings (ruled-line backgrounds); where I measured by hand, tokens pass (body `#2b2118` ≈ 14:1, muted `#5a4a3a` ≈ 7–8:1, accent `#962f1b` on cream ≈ 7:1). I found no sub-6:1 text. Dark mode held up in the places I looked.
- No sideways page scroll at 375 or 390 anywhere (`scrollWidth == innerWidth` on every screen I opened, including jam, editor, compare, spaces).

## Findings

Fixed since: #8, #9 and #10 (2026-10-15; see "Grammar-text fixes" in docs/design.md); #2 and #7 (2026-10-15: on phones the jam bar is a one-line strip — code, role, avatars, the host's phase button, ▾ — with everything else behind the expander; everyone gets a name prompt on joining and a stable placeholder like "Brisk Moth" until they type one, used in the bar, comments, presence tags and reveal cards).
Also fixed 2026-10-15: #4 (Keep toast says "Kept · on your shelf" with an Open shelf link, the shelf fold flashes and shows a count), #12 (space name field styled), #16 (Add to bank always enabled, says what's missing, slots named by their frame), #18 (Copy link: share sheet on phones, else clipboard, else a selectable field), #19 (toasts move to the top when a field sits where they'd land), #20 (shuffles say what moved), #21 (own-word bank step folds into the piece step; pins are steps with a label).

## Not verified

- Soft keyboard: the sheet's "Your own word…" field and the jam comment box sit at the bottom edge of a bottom-anchored sheet. A real phone keyboard probably covers them unless the sheet lifts with `visualViewport`. Please check on a device. *Handled in 608d534 (the picker and Rules sheets lift by `--kb` from `visualViewport` and shrink to the room left; the focused field scrolls into view); still unverified on a real keyboard.*
- Real-clipboard behaviour, share sheet, and iOS Safari address-bar resize (the fixed Shuffle bar could jump).
- Landscape orientation.
- Space features beyond create/delete (publish, jam in space, export/import).

## What worked well

- First visit: the "tap to change" bubble and the pulsing first word make the first action obvious; the hint fades in and dismisses; "Tap any word to step it from plain to strange" stays under the piece.
- Tuning: the sheet keeps the tapped word visible above it (scrolls the page; verified for a 3-line word) and the headline words are 43px tall. ‹ ›, Surprise, Pin ("Pinned" survives a shuffle), your-own-word, and Undo/Redo all behave, with a clear toast ("Redid: added “sneakily damp” to Who · descriptor").
- Challenges: move counter, a clear refusal at the limit ("That would be move 4 of 3. Put something back first."), compare cards that swipe with the next card peeking, and survival of reload.
- Jams: link toasts say exactly what each link allows; together-mode edits and comments sync within ~1s; workshop round → send → reveal → claps worked end to end; the host delete reaches guests ("The host deleted this jam.").
- Destructive actions are recoverable: forks and kept items give "Undo brings it back"; jam and space delete ask first.
- Layout: no sideways scroll at 375/390; dark mode legible with the same hierarchy; History is a clean full-screen sheet.
- Text quality: Story Bits and the poems read well overall; the pronoun agreement ("they are … have commissioned", "he … his") held in every sample.
